Dboi

15 years. Conventions coast to coast. Now rooted in Queens, NYC — creating work that lasts a lifetime.

Starting in a small studio, I worked my way through conventions across the USA, building relationships with clients and artists nationwide. That journey led me to open StayOnPoint Tattoo Studio — a space where I specialize in photorealistic portraits and large-scale black and grey work, and where traveling artists from around the world come to showcase their talent in one of the most vibrant cities on earth.

The relationships built through this art form — with clients, fellow artists, and the global tattoo community — remain the most rewarding part of this journey.
